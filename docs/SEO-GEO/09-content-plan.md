## 📏 CONTENT QUALITY STANDARDS

### For pillar articles (2000+ words)
- Original data/research
- Expert author bio
- Source citations
- FAQ section with schema
- TL;DR + direct answer
- Tables and lists
- Original images with alt
- Internal links to 3+ other pages
- External links to 2+ authoritative sources

### For supporting articles (800-1500 words)
- Clear topic focus
- Direct answer at top
- 2-3 subsections
- 1-2 internal links
- Schema: Article + FAQPage (if FAQ)
- Author attribution

### For news/announcements
- 400-800 words
- Date prominent
- Clear news value
- 1-2 images
- 1 CTA
