# PHASE 7: GEO STRATEGY (Generative Engine Optimization)

**Goal:** Get cited by AI Search (Perplexity, ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews)  
**Date:** 2026-09-06

---

## 🎯 GEO OBJECTIVES

1. **Be citable** — AI engines trust the entity
2. **Be findable** — Visible in AI search results
3. **Be quotable** — Has factual, attributed statements
4. **Be verifiable** — Source attribution for claims

---

## 📋 GEO OPTIMIZATION PILLARS

### Pillar 1: Direct Answers
- Every page should have 1-2 sentence direct answer at top
- Answer the page's main query immediately
- Example: "Mầm non Bình Minh là trường mầm non tư thục tại Vũng Tàu..."

### Pillar 2: Factual Entity Profile
- Name, address, phone, hours, programs
- All verified, all consistent
- On website, Wikidata, GBP, everywhere

### Pillar 3: Structured Information
- Use schema.org
- Use clear HTML structure
- Use lists and tables
- Use semantic headings (H1-H6)

### Pillar 4: Source Attribution
- Every factual claim → link to source
- "Last updated" date
- Author bio on articles
- "Reviewed by" if applicable

### Pillar 5: Consistent Entities
- Same name, address, phone everywhere
- Wikidata Q141329654 as anchor
- Cross-link to GBP, Facebook, etc.

## 🎯 AI CITATION TRIGGERS

### What makes AI cite a source
1. **Clear authorship** — "According to [Author]"
2. **Direct answers** — "X is Y"
3. **Source attribution** — "Source: [URL]"
4. **Recency** — Updated date visible
5. **Authority signals** — Schema, citations, expert author
6. **Structured data** — Tables, lists, definitions
7. **Unique data** — Original statistics, research

## ⚠️ GEO PITFALLS

1. **No clear answers** — AI can't quote vague content
2. **Contradicting sources** — NAP inconsistency breaks trust
3. **Outdated info** — Reduces citation likelihood
4. **Plagiarized content** — AI detects and avoids
5. **No schema** — Less likely to be understood
6. **Inaccessible to crawlers** — robots.txt blocks AI
7. **Thin content** — AI prefers substantial sources
